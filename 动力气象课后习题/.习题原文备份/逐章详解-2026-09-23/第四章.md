9. 证明 $p$ 坐标系中水平运动方程可改写为以下通量形式 $$ \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+\frac{\partial u^2}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial uv}{\partial y}\right)_p+\frac{\partial \omega u}{\partial p}-fv=-\left(\frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial x}\right)_p $$ $$ \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial t}+\frac{\partial uv}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial v^2}{\partial y}\right)_p+\frac{\partial \omega v}{\partial p}+fu=-\left(\frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial y}\right)_p $$
10. 证明 $p$ 坐标系中水平运动方程可改写为以下形式 $$ \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\right)_p-(\zeta_p+f)v+\delta_p u+\frac{\partial \omega u}{\partial p}=\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial x}\right)_p $$ $$ \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial t}\right)_p+(\zeta_p+f)u+\delta_p v+\frac{\partial \omega v}{\partial p}=\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)_p $$其中：$\zeta_p=\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right)_p+\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)_p$，是 $p$ 坐标系中铅直涡度； $\delta_p=\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)_p+\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right)_p$，是 $p$ 坐标系中水平散度； $P=\Phi+\frac{u^2+v^2}{2}$，是位能与动能之和。
