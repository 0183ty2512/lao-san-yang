Wind profiles under different thermal stratification cases.

Wind Profiles and Thermal Stratification

The behavior of wind profiles differs dramatically under convective buoyant and stable conditions, which suppress turbulence. If one is at a reference height some distance above a crop or forest, wind speeds will be greater at the canopy interface under convective conditions than during stable night-time conditions. Evidence comes from everyday experience when one feels a diminishment of wind after sunset.
